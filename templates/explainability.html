C. Interpretability Limitations & Machine Learning Integrity
Essential academic principles distinguishing statistical association from real-world causality
⚠️ Importance ≠ Causation
A high feature importance score indicates that splitting on this feature produced substantial Gini impurity reduction across tree nodes. It does not prove an isolated physical cause-and-effect relationship in real marketplace transactions.
📊 High-Cardinality MDI Bias
Standard Mean Decrease in Impurity (MDI) can slightly favor continuous variables or categorical features with many unique levels (such as Brand with 29 unique categories). Aggregating one-hot columns groups related splits into honest, holistic feature totals.
🎯 Explained Variance vs Accuracy
The test metric R² = 0.5780 measures the proportion of market price variance captured by the 7 features. It is not a percentage accuracy score, and cannot account for unrecorded vehicle condition or regional tax nuances.
