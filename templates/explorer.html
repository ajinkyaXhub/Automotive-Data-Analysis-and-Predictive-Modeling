Data Integrity, Missing Values & Outlier Handling

1. Missing Values: The raw CarDekho CSV contained 0 null values across all 8 columns. The dataset schema was validated prior to model training.

2. Deduplication: Exactly 763 duplicate transaction records were detected and purged during data preparation, reducing raw rows from 4,340 to 3,577. This step is critical to prevent data leakage between training and testing splits.

3. Outlier Handling: Extreme high-value vehicles (e.g. BMW, Audi, Land Rover exceeding ₹ 50 Lakhs) and high-mileage cars (> 5,00,000 km) represent legitimate transactions in the Indian automobile market. They are preserved in the training dataset and visualized truthfully above without synthetic clipping or artificial smoothing.
